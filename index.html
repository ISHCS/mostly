Roles & Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Architecture

The Bishoftu Portal implements strict security profiles to ensure operational integrity across quarry sites and head offices in Ethiopia.

Site Manager
Admin / Full Access

Complete control over employee credentials, all operational logs, financial vouchers, approvals, and database backups.

Fleet Supervisor
Equipment & Fuel Focus

Manages heavy machinery operational logs, dump truck cycle counts, fuel distribution, and maintenance requests.

Finance Manager
Petty Cash & Expenses

Controls petty cash disbursements, site expenses, haulage income, and approves purchase requisitions.

Machine Operator
Field Logging

Submits daily machine work hours, fuel consumption requests, and production volumes from the field.
